Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

1. Who we are

Frozen 3D Studio Poster is a personal tool operated by the owner of the TikTok account @frozen3dstudio. Contact: mrozowsski@gmail.com.

2. Data we access

When the owner logs in with TikTok, the app receives an access token and the account's open ID, and may read basic creator information needed to publish (for example, allowed privacy settings). No data about other TikTok users is collected.

3. How data is used

The token is used only to publish the owner's own approved posts to the owner's own account. Data is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used for analytics.

4. Storage

The token is stored locally on the owner's computer in a configuration file. Media files are uploaded temporarily to a storage bucket for publishing and removed afterwards.

5. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while the app is in use. The owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok settings (Security and permissions, Apps and services) and delete the stored token. For any request, write to mrozowsski@gmail.com.

6. Changes

This policy may be updated. The current version is always available at this address.
